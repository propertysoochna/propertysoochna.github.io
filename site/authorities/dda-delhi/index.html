Delhi Development Authority

DDA housing schemes, flats and booking updates

DDA public-flat schemes and qualifying government residential plots in Delhi. A flat offer count is not a verified live remaining balance.

Property Soochna provides independent, source-linked discovery and updates. This page is not the DDA website and does not imply affiliation.

Official portals

  • https://dda.gov.in/
  • https://eservices.dda.org.in/

What to check before applying

  1. Identify the scheme and pocket, then read the current DDA brochure and any extension or additional-inventory circular together.
  2. Check category eligibility, registration versus booking dates, booking amount, maintenance and other charges before using the DDA booking portal.
  3. First-come-first-served booking and draw/e-auction processes are different; use the method and conditions in the specific scheme notice.

DDA Karmajeevi Awaas Yojana 2026 — Pocket 11

DDA public flats, with occupation/professional eligibility restrictions; not government plots.

Location: Pocket 11, Sector A1–A4, Narela. Block X: 1-BHK, 60.86–61.34 m² published area including balcony/common area; carpet area: Not recorded. Parking area 5.76 m².

Published price and charges: Block X: ₹33.40–33.66 lakh after 25% discount. Add ₹75,000 corpus, 12 months’ maintenance at ₹1.50/sq ft/month and ₹2,000 water. Known subtotal about ₹34.31–34.57 lakh including ₹2,500 new-user registration, before conversion, stamp/registration, applicable taxes, finance and legal/site costs.

Deposit or upfront payment: ₹2,500 registration including GST, non-refundable; existing Awaas registrants need not pay again. Booking: ₹50,000 for 1-BHK, ₹4 lakh for 2-BHK or ₹10 lakh for 3-BHK; credited once, but forfeitable on surrender/cancellation/default.

Who can apply: Indian citizens aged 18+, PAN and own savings account; qualifying serving/retired regular government-sector personnel, or professionally qualified salaried/self-employed people. Government ID/employer proof plus salary slip/Form16, or professional degree plus professional registration/ITR/employment proof as prescribed. Existing property ownership is allowed.

Published dates: Original booking opened 15 August 2026 at 12:00 IST; Block X opened 11 October at 12:00 IST. The 30 October circular extends booking to 31 December 2026, subject to available flats. Exact closing hour: Not recorded.

Important: Eligibility is restricted to qualifying government personnel and professionals. Remaining flats are visible only after login; offered lists are not unsold balances. MEDIUM risk: as-is condition, final unit charges and resale evidence need checking. No transfer before registered conveyance; booking is not refundable on surrender/default.

Official evidence: 30 October extension / Block X circular / Brochure / FAQ / 544-flat release / Awaas entry

DDA Karmayogi Awaas Yojana 2025

DDA public flats under FCFS, restricted to qualifying government-related employees and retirees; not plots.

Location: Pockets 6, 9 and 13, Sector A1–A4, Narela. Original Pocket 9 annexure: 1-BHK 61.17–61.65 m²; 2-BHK 126.53–140.56 m²; 3-BHK 163.76–183.36 m² published plinth area including common space. These are not carpet areas.

Published price and charges: Original Pocket 9 discounted ranges: ₹34.03–34.28 lakh for 1-BHK, ₹79.81–88.16 lakh for 2-BHK and ₹114.57–127.11 lakh for 3-BHK. They are not quotes for every later release. Add corpus ₹75,000/₹1.5 lakh/₹2.5 lakh respectively, 12 months’ maintenance at ₹1.50/₹2/₹2.50 per sq ft per month, ₹2,000 water and applicable conversion, tax and deed charges. A fresh-registration Pocket 9 1-BHK known subtotal is about ₹34.94–35.20 lakh before those unquantified extras.

Deposit or upfront payment: ₹2,500 including GST, non-refundable, unless already registered on Awaas. Booking ₹50,000/₹4 lakh/₹10 lakh for 1/2/3-BHK respectively, credited against price once but forfeitable on surrender/default.

Who can apply: Indian citizens aged 18+, serving or retired government servants including Central/State government, PSU, public-sector bank, local/autonomous-body and university personnel within the brochure’s categories; PAN and own savings account required. Existing property ownership and multiple purchases are permitted. Preserve employment/retirement and category proof.

Published dates: Registration began 19 December 2025; booking began 14 January 2026. The 30 October 2026 circular supersedes the earlier 31 October cutoff and extends booking to 31 December 2026, subject to stock. Exact cutoff hour: Not recorded.

Important: MEDIUM risk: final unit availability and demand require checking after login. Do not import Karmajeevi’s newer interest wording: this brochure says that a shortfall after 60 days can attract 10% annual simple interest on the entire amount for the entire month. Obtain the applicable demand and pay in full within 60 days if proceeding. No transfer before the registered conveyance deed; financing does not extend the deadline.

Official evidence: 30 October extension / 29 September extension / Full brochure and annexure / Awaas scheme entry

DDA Janta Awaas Yojana Phase II 2026

DDA-administered public EWS draw in the DLF Urban Pvt Ltd-built Pragati 1 project. This is developer-built housing administered by DDA, not a government plot, a DDA-built flat or a TCP e-Draw offer.

Location: Rohtak Road near Jakhira Flyover, New Delhi. Published plinth area 61.48–62.77 m² including common area, plus 7.08 m² scooter parking; carpet area: Not recorded.

Published price and charges: ₹34.86–35.50 lakh excluding GST. Brochure clause 10 says the disposal price already includes water, maintenance, corpus/sinking fund and DDA processing fee—do not add them a second time. Including the registration fee gives ₹34.885–35.525 lakh before GST, stamp/registration, finance and legal/site costs; it is not an all-in ceiling.

Deposit or upfront payment: ₹2,500 including GST, non-refundable and scheme-specific even for an existing DDA registrant. No separate application EMD is stated in the reviewed brochure. A successful applicant later pays the developer under its Demand-cum-Allotment Letter.

Who can apply: Indian citizens aged 18+ with PAN and their own savings account. Applicant and co-applicant families must each separately have annual income no more than ₹10 lakh; family income means applicant plus spouse. Valid competent-authority income/EWS certificates must be issued by the registration cutoff. A co-applicant must be family; reserved categories require the prescribed proof. Delhi property ownership is not prohibited.

Published dates: Registration 7 October–7 December 2026; closing hour: Not recorded. Proposed computerized draw: 15 December 2026, not a guaranteed actual draw date.

Important: HIGH resale risk: a five-year resale lock-in runs from physical possession. DLF Urban issues the demand, receives the price and executes the sale deed; do not assume DDA is the seller. No flat/floor choice is guaranteed. Completion, the particular flat’s condition, GST demand and deed valuation need checking. The 209 advertised flats are draw inventory, not an FCFS balance.

Official evidence: Launch circular / Current full brochure / Official application entry

Common questions

Where should I check DDA property opportunities?

Start with Delhi Development Authority's official portals below. Use this guide and the dated bulletin to understand the published findings, then verify the current notice.

Are the schemes shown open today?

Not necessarily. Status, dates and risks below are from the published check. A closed scheme or a notice needing clarification is not an open application. Balances may be login-gated, and later notices can change the terms.

What are the eligibility rules, deposit and application route?

Use the named scheme below, not an authority-wide assumption. Each published finding retains its eligibility, price, upfront-payment and date conditions with the official evidence. A reserve or base price is not the final cost. If a term needs clarification, confirm it with the issuer before paying.

Can PropertySoochna apply or take payment for me?

No. We discover and explain opportunities; we do not transact, book, negotiate or process applications. Apply and pay only through the issuing authority's officially prescribed route.
