Delhi Development Authority

DDA housing schemes, flats and booking updates

DDA public-flat schemes and qualifying government residential plots in Delhi. A flat offer count is not a verified live remaining balance.

Property Soochna provides independent, source-linked discovery and updates. This page is not the DDA website and does not imply affiliation.

Official portals

  • https://dda.gov.in/
  • https://eservices.dda.org.in/

What to check before applying

  1. Identify the scheme and pocket, then read the current DDA brochure and any extension or additional-inventory circular together.
  2. Check category eligibility, registration versus booking dates, booking amount, maintenance and other charges before using the DDA booking portal.
  3. First-come-first-served booking and draw/e-auction processes are different; use the method and conditions in the specific scheme notice.

DDA Karmayogi Awaas Yojana 2025

DDA public flats under FCFS, restricted to qualifying government-related employees and retirees; not plots.

Location: Pockets 6, 9 and 13, Sector A1–A4, Narela. Original Pocket 9 annexure: 1-BHK 61.17–61.65 m²; 2-BHK 126.53–140.56 m²; 3-BHK 163.76–183.36 m² published plinth area including common space. These are not carpet areas.

Published dates: Registration began 19 December 2025; booking began 14 January 2026. The 29 September 2026 circular extends booking to 31 October 2026; exact cutoff hour: Not recorded. Stock may exhaust earlier.

Important: MEDIUM risk: final unit availability and demand require checking after login. Do not import Karmajeevi’s newer interest wording: this brochure says that a shortfall after 60 days can attract 10% annual simple interest on the entire amount for the entire month. Obtain the applicable demand and pay in full within 60 days if proceeding. No transfer before the registered conveyance deed; financing does not extend the deadline.

Official evidence: 29 September extension / Full brochure and annexure / Awaas scheme entry

Common questions

Where should I check DDA property opportunities?

Start with Delhi Development Authority's official portals below. Use this guide and the dated bulletin to understand the published findings, then verify the current notice.

Are the schemes shown open today?

Not necessarily. Status, dates and risks below are from the published check. A closed scheme or a notice needing clarification is not an open application. Balances may be login-gated, and later notices can change the terms.

Can PropertySoochna apply or take payment for me?

No. We discover and explain opportunities; we do not transact, book, negotiate or process applications. Apply and pay only through the issuing authority's officially prescribed route.
