GDA Ghaziabad residential plot auctions and notices

Individual government residential plots in Ghaziabad, including qualifying Indirapuram lots. Commercial and developer group-housing parcels are separate.

What to check before applying

  1. Match the notice number, revised lot schedule and every applicable corrigendum before choosing a plot.
  2. Separate reserve price from the eventual winning bid. Check EMD, application fee, surcharges, location premiums and the selected payment/possession plan.
  3. Confirm registration and auction cutoffs through the official notice. A published list does not prove a lot remains available.

Common questions

Where should I check GDA Ghaziabad property opportunities?

Start with Ghaziabad Development Authority's official portals below. Use this guide and the dated bulletin to understand the published findings, then verify the current notice.

Are the schemes shown open today?

Not necessarily. Status, dates and risks below are from the published check. A closed scheme or a notice needing clarification is not an open application. Balances may be login-gated, and later notices can change the terms.

Can PropertySoochna apply or take payment for me?

No. We discover and explain opportunities; we do not transact, book, negotiate or process applications. Apply and pay only through the issuing authority's officially prescribed route.
