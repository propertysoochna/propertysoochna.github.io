Official-source discovery

Government property schemes, by authority.

Find the issuing authority, its official portals and the regional coverage to read before acting.

Property Soochna (PropertySoochna) is an independent discovery and monitoring publication, not a government service, broker or application agent.

This directory lists 62 source references, not 62 verified sellers or live schemes. A planning, pending or policy reference does not confirm a public sale. No nationwide live-inventory claim is made.

Read an authority guide.

These six guides explain the NCR authority streams and link to the dated published findings. Other regions retain their existing source-directory pages.

DDA housing schemes, flats and booking updates

DDA public-flat schemes and qualifying government residential plots in Delhi. A flat offer count is not a verified live remaining balance.

GDA Ghaziabad residential plot auctions and notices

Individual government residential plots in Ghaziabad, including qualifying Indirapuram lots. Commercial and developer group-housing parcels are separate.

HSVP HUDA residential plot auctions and notices

Qualifying general-public residential plots in Haryana urban estates. Restricted categories and non-residential auctions are not interchangeable.

MDA Meerut residential plot auctions and results

Meerut Development Authority individual residential plot opportunities, with closed auctions and results distinguished from new applications.

YEIDA residential plot schemes, draws and notices

YEIDA government residential leasehold plots in the Yamuna Expressway/Jewar catchment. YEIDA, NOIDA Authority and Greater Noida Authority are different issuers.

TCP Haryana affordable housing draws and DDJAY plots

Regulated-private builder affordable flats and licensed DDJAY residential plots. These are not government-owned homes or authority allotments.
