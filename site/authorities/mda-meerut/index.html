Meerut Development Authority

MDA Meerut residential plot auctions and results

Meerut Development Authority individual residential plot opportunities, with closed auctions and results distinguished from new applications.

What to check before applying

  1. Read the advertisement and residential-property list; do not treat commercial or group-housing parcels as individual residential plots.
  2. Check bidder registration, EMD/payment and document cutoffs separately from the auction date; they may fall earlier.
  3. A past auction result is not a new sale. Verify a new public notice before assuming registration has reopened.
