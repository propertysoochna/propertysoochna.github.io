TCP Haryana affordable housing draws and DDJAY plots

Regulated-private builder affordable flats and licensed DDJAY residential plots. These are not government-owned homes or authority allotments.

Property Soochna provides independent, source-linked discovery and updates. This page is not the TCP Haryana website and does not imply affiliation.

  • https://haryanarera.gov.in/

What to check before applying

  1. For affordable flats, match the project ID, developer, location, notice, application period and draw/result entry on the official e-Draw system.
  2. For DDJAY plots, match the TCP licence and RERA/project records. Regulatory approval alone does not establish an open sale or current price.
  3. Retain deadline conflicts, unverified charges and material application blockers. Title, site, finance and purchase diligence remain the buyer's responsibility.

Are the schemes shown open today?

Not necessarily. Status, dates and risks below are from the published check. A closed scheme or a notice needing clarification is not an open application. Balances may be login-gated, and later notices can change the terms.

No. We discover and explain opportunities; we do not transact, book, negotiate or process applications. Apply and pay only through the issuing authority's officially prescribed route.
