What changed

  • HSVP, YEIDA and DDA had no open or confirmed-upcoming qualifying plot scheme.
  • GDA Notice 715 registration had already closed.
  • MDA's cheapest verified group had a reserve cost of Rs 19.01 lakh per plot.
  • Anantya was clearly labelled as private-builder housing, not government-owned property.

Decision note

The baseline established the separation between authority-owned plots and TCP-regulated private-builder flats. Availability in one stream was never used to imply availability in another.

Cost and risk note

Reserve and displayed prices were not treated as final acquisition costs. Premiums, authority charges, registration, finance and verification remained separate.
