MDA's 67-property auction entered its urgent deadline window

8 September 2026

MDA Meerut remained the only qualifying government residential plot opportunity. TCP Haryana Project 230, Anantya, also remained open as a separate private-builder flat stream.

This early bulletin focused on plots and TCP Haryana e-Draw housing.

TCP Haryana e-Draw1 open

Anantya, Project ID 230, offered 680 private-builder flats in Faridabad.

What changed

  • The safest MDA payment and EMD deadline was 9 September at noon.
  • The cheapest verified MDA plots were 86.4 sqm MIG-1 plots at a reserve cost of Rs 19.01 lakh.
  • Anantya displayed a proposed draw date of 27 October 2026.
  • Y CON Platinum Heights published evaluated, winner and wait-list results.

Decision note

The MDA booklet and portal displayed different deadline times. The earlier official noon deadline was used as the safe deadline rather than relying on the portal's later time.

Cost and risk note

The MDA reserve price excluded potential location premium, freehold charges, utilities, stamp duty, registration, finance and legal or site-verification costs.
