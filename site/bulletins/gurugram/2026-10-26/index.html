Tathastu 35 - Project 228, second e-draw

Property
Regulated-private affordable flats from Tathastu Realty Private Limited, not government-owned housing. Five originally offered two-bedroom-plus-store units; the portal's study-room flag is No.
Location
Sector 35, Sohna, Gurugram district. The signed licence places the property in the revenue estates of Mohammadpur Gujjar and Sohna. Carpet area is 645.73 sq ft; the three offered types have balconies of 62.97, 87.30 or 106.89 sq ft.
Price
Published rates are Rs 5,450 per carpet sq ft and Rs 1,300 per balcony sq ft. Calculated base prices range from Rs 36,01,089.50 to Rs 36,58,185.50, not all-in prices. GST, maintenance/IFMS, utilities, stamp duty, registration and finance costs are not recorded.
Upfront payment
Rs 1,75,961.43 deposit plus Rs 590 registration fee, totalling Rs 1,76,551.43. The deposit equals 5% of the carpet-price component, not 5% of the carpet-plus-balcony total. Full payment tranches, cancellation deductions and refund terms are not recorded.
Who can apply
A PMAY certificate is mandatory if claiming that category. The published preference affidavits require family/property declarations, no duplicate application in the same colony and retention of only one affordable-policy flat. Complete age, citizenship, income and preference-priority conditions are not recorded; do not assume every applicant is eligible.
Dates
Advertised opening: 24 October 2026, 08:00 IST. Payment closes 7 November 2026, 23:59 IST; application submission closes 9 November 2026, 23:59 IST. Use the earlier payment cutoff when assessing the application window. Proposed draw: 30 November 2026, 16:00 IST. Project details were checked on 24 October; the listed window and licence documents were checked again on 26 October.

Offered units: Type 1C-FF(MQ): one unit; Type 2A: three units; Type 2B: one unit. Unit numbers, floor-level availability and MQ release conditions are not recorded.

Important: High risk until the full payment, refund, possession and transfer terms are clear. Only two-wheeler parking at 0.5 ECS per dwelling is described; do not assume a car space is included. TCP licence 131 of 2023, dated 23 June 2023, covers 9.90625 acres and the current table gives validity to 22 June 2028. The current table names Tathastu Realty, while the original grant names Sedulous Realtech; the intervening legal-entity documentation needs confirmation. The e-Draw page prints RERA 108 of 2023, dated 4 December 2023; current independent RERA validity, progress and adverse-order checks are not established. The available preference affidavits are not a complete sales brochure. These verified notice facts do not establish an application-ready offer, and five offered units are not a live remaining balance.

More details

The base-price calculation is carpet area times the carpet rate, plus balcony area times the balcony rate. The three calculated bases are Rs 36,01,089.50, Rs 36,32,718.50 and Rs 36,58,185.50 respectively. These exclude the unverified additional costs listed above. Earlier linked Project 169 is not a second new opportunity.
