Closed

RPS10/2026 — closed residential-plot scheme

Property
YEIDA government residential leasehold plots offered by draw; historical applicant/allottee information, not a new sale.
Location
YEIDA Sectors 15C, 18 and 24A in the Greater Noida/Jewar catchment. Do not describe these as NOIDA Authority plots.
Price
Historical allotment rate ₹36,260/m²: base ₹58.74–105.15 lakh for the original 162–290 m² sizes. Add the brochure's one-time lease rent of 10% and applicable location premiums, deed, development/utility and finance costs. Historical location premiums are 5% each for park-facing, corner and a road of at least 18 m, capped at 15%. These are base prices, not all-in acquisition costs or current resale quotations.
Upfront payment
Historical form cost ₹600. Original registration deposits were ₹5,87,412–₹10,51,540 for general applicants and ₹2,93,706–₹5,25,770 for SC/ST applicants across the advertised sizes. Successful deposits adjust against premium once. The original brochure required the full premium within 60 days of the allotment letter; the later existing-allottee extension is described below. These are not instructions to pay a new application deposit today.
Who can apply
No new applicant window is established. Original general eligibility required Indian applicants/co-applicants aged at least 18 at scheme issue, legally competent and not prohibited from buying property in Uttar Pradesh; one plot application, no HUF, and prior YEIDA allotment/family/immediate-family co-applicant restrictions applied. Spouses both allotted had to surrender one within one month under the brochure's conditions. Reserved farmer/functional-industrial categories had separate conditions. The 7% land-acquisition allotments are separately restricted to identified beneficiaries.
Dates
Original application 6 April–6 May 2026; advertised draw 18 June 2026, not confirmation of the actual draw date. The later existing-allottee payment extension ended 29 September 2026; exact cutoff time: Not recorded. Original brochure terms were decoded on 24 October; the unchanged brochure and payment notice were retrieved on 26 October at 23:04:50 IST, and the result entry at 23:05:32 IST.

Offered units: 973 original plots: 476 of 162 m², four each of 183 and 184 m², 481 of 200 m², six of 223 m² and two of 290 m²; not available stock.

Important: HIGH risk for a presumed quick resale: this is a 90-year lease, not unrestricted freehold. The original as-is brochure anticipated about five years for development before possession and required construction/occupancy within three years of the lease deed. No subdivision/amalgamation; the first three construction-extension years carried charges of 4%, 5% and 6% of total premium. Transfers require authority permission and conditions; reserved farmer/functional-industrial allotments had five-year transfer restrictions. Post-lease surrender deducted 30% of premium. Current transfer permission, construction compliance, lease arrears and the exact plot's title/site require checking before a resale transaction. The payment notice allowed payment through 29 September with 13.50% annual interest compounded half-yearly and referred to cancellation proceedings under clause 19(iii). It does not prove that every defaulting allotment was actually cancelled. The brochure prints RERA UPRERAPRJ307764/03/2026; independent current RERA verification is not established. New availability is not established by the surviving brochure or result page.

Results and refunds

RPS10/2026 existing applicants and allottees: use the official result entry privately and match your own allotment/demand. The payment notice concerns the expired 29 September extension, not a new application or payment window. For any post-deadline payment, obtain written YEIDA confirmation of the allotment status and valid official payment route. No personal lookup was performed; individual outcomes, actual draw occurrence, waitlist movement and refund releases are not confirmed.
