Yamuna Expressway Industrial Development Authority

Closed

RPS10/2026 - closed application window

Property
Government-authority residential plots on 90-year leases. The original brochure offered 973 plots; this is historical offered stock, not the current unsold balance.
Location
Sectors 15C, 18 and 24A along the Yamuna Expressway. Original plot sizes were 162, 183, 184, 200, 223 and 290 sq m.
Price
Historical base rate Rs 36,260 per sq m, giving Rs 58,74,120 to Rs 1,05,15,400 for 162-290 sq m before extras. One-time lease rent is 10% of the plot cost; location premiums are 5% each for a park/green belt, corner or road at least 18 m wide, capped at 15%. A 162 sq m illustration is Rs 64,61,532 including 10% lease rent but before location premiums, stamp duty, registration, taxes, revisions and construction/finance costs. These are not current sale quotations or all-in prices.
Upfront payment
Original 162 sq m registration amount: Rs 5,87,412 for other applicants or Rs 2,93,706 for SC/ST applicants; form cost Rs 600. Registration money was adjustable against the premium for successful applicants. The original brochure required the full premium within 60 days of allotment-letter issue. The later payment order applies to the earlier scheme; these historical figures are not an invitation to make a new application payment.
Who can apply
The original scheme required applicants/co-applicants aged at least 18, competent to contract and legally eligible to buy in Uttar Pradesh. HUFs were ineligible. One-application, prior-YEIDA-allotment, family and immediate-family co-applicant restrictions applied; spouses both allotted had to surrender one under the brochure's conditions. Farmer and functional-industrial-unit reservations had separate eligibility. A new application window is not established.
Dates
Original application window: 6 April to 6 May 2026. The brochure proposed a draw on 18 June; that is not confirmation of the actual draw date. The later payment extension ended on 29 September 2026, with no exact cutoff time stated. The brochure/result entry was checked on 24 October; the payment-order text and later notice metadata were checked on 26 October.

Offered units: Original brochure: 476 plots of 162 sq m; four of 183 sq m; four of 184 sq m; 481 of 200 sq m; six of 223 sq m; two of 290 sq m. These add to 973, not current availability.

Important: This is a closed historical offer, not an October reopening. The payment notice requires 13.50% annual interest calculated on a half-yearly-compounding basis and warns of cancellation proceedings under condition 19(iii) for non-payment; it does not establish that every unpaid allotment has already been cancelled. The brochure describes as-is-where-is plots and approximately five years of development before possession, with construction and occupancy certification within three years of the lease deed. No subdivision/amalgamation; transfer requires authority permission and conditions, with a five-year restriction for specified reserved categories. The printed RERA number UPRERAPRJ307764/03/2026 was not independently verified against current RERA records.

Original RPS10/2026 brochure, payment-extension order, official result entry.
More details

Original registration amounts for SC/ST applicants and other applicants respectively were: 162 sq m, Rs 2,93,706 / Rs 5,87,412; 183 sq m, Rs 3,31,779 / Rs 6,63,558; 184 sq m, Rs 3,33,592 / Rs 6,67,184; 200 sq m, Rs 3,62,600 / Rs 7,25,200; 223 sq m, Rs 4,04,299 / Rs 8,08,598; 290 sq m, Rs 5,25,770 / Rs 10,51,540.

The original application method was online through the authority's payment gateway/net banking, not offline payment. It is not a verified current application route. Existing applicants should use the official result entry and their own allotment/demand documents, rather than the expired application or extension dates.

The brochure's first three construction-extension years carry charges of 4%, 5% and 6% of total premium. Its post-lease surrender clause deducts 30% of total premium. Confirm which clauses and later orders apply to a particular allotment; do not infer current possession or transfer permission from the historical brochure.

Results and refunds

RPS10/2026 applicants: the official draw-result entry has an application-number search. No personal result was looked up. Official archive metadata displays 30 June 2026 while an allotment-check card displays 6 July; neither is asserted here as the actual draw or first-publication date. Winner totals, individual outcomes and refund-release dates are not recorded. The payment extension described above expired on 29 September; it is not an extra October payment window.

Village Thora, Tehsil Jewar - 7% plot final list: the authority's official archive lists the final-list announcement dated 12 September 2026 and links its notice. The listing and link were checked on 26 October, not the personal table or underlying entitlement clauses. This is restricted-beneficiary information, not a general-public plot application. Individual entitlement, objection deadlines and refund instructions are not recorded.

Information checked on 26 October 2026. Availability, inventory, prices and deadlines may change. Confirm current details with the issuing authority before applying or paying.
