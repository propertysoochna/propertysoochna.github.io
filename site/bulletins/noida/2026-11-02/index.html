Source wording & documents Full recorded facts
Original brochure / Existing-allottee payment notice / Public result entry / Current residential scheme page
Status

Closed

Property

YEIDA government residential leasehold plots offered by draw; historical applicant/allottee information, not a new sale.

Location

YEIDA Sectors 15C, 18 and 24A in the Greater Noida/Jewar catchment. Do not describe these as NOIDA Authority plots.

Price

Historical allotment rate ₹36,260/m²: base ₹58.74–105.15 lakh for the original 162–290 m² sizes. Add the brochure’s one-time lease rent of 10% and applicable location premiums, deed, development/utility and finance costs. These are not current resale quotations.

Upfront payment

Historical form cost ₹600. Original registration deposits were ₹5,87,412–₹10,51,540 for general applicants and ₹2,93,706–₹5,25,770 for SC/ST applicants across the advertised sizes. Successful deposits adjust against premium once. The original brochure required the full premium within 60 days of the allotment letter; the later existing-allottee extension is described below. These are not instructions to pay a new application deposit today.

Who can apply

No new applicant window is established. Original general eligibility required Indian applicants/co-applicants aged at least 18 at scheme issue, legally competent and not prohibited from buying property in Uttar Pradesh; one plot application, no HUF, and prior YEIDA allotment/family/co-applicant restrictions applied. Reserved farmer/functional-industrial categories had separate conditions. The 7% land-acquisition allotments are separately restricted to identified beneficiaries.

Dates

Original application 6 April–6 May 2026; advertised draw 18 June 2026. The later existing-allottee payment extension ended 29 September 2026.

Important

This is a 90-year lease, not unrestricted freehold. The original as-is brochure anticipated about five years for development before possession, required construction/occupancy within three years of the lease deed, and imposed authority transfer conditions. The payment notice allowed payment through 29 September with 13.50% annual interest compounded half-yearly and referred to cancellation proceedings under clause 19(iii). It does not prove that every defaulting allotment was actually cancelled. New availability is not established by the surviving brochure or result page.

Official source

Original brochure / Existing-allottee payment notice / Public result entry / Current residential scheme page

Offered units

973 original plots: 476 of 162 m², four each of 183 and 184 m², 481 of 200 m², six of 223 m² and two of 290 m²; not available stock.

Steps and supporting details

Existing applicants should use the official result entry privately and match their own allotment/demand. For any post-deadline payment, obtain written YEIDA confirmation of the allotment status and valid official payment route. Historical PLC is 5% each for park-facing, corner and a road of at least 18 m, capped at 15%; deposits count toward dues once. Current transfer permission, construction compliance, lease arrears and the exact plot’s title/site require checking before a resale transaction.

Original deposit schedule (general / SC-ST): 162 m² ₹5,87,412 / ₹2,93,706; 183 m² ₹6,63,558 / ₹3,31,779; 184 m² ₹6,67,184 / ₹3,33,592; 200 m² ₹7,25,200 / ₹3,62,600; 223 m² ₹8,08,598 / ₹4,04,299; 290 m² ₹10,51,540 / ₹5,25,770. The historical application route was online through YEIDA, not offline payments.

The original terms prohibited subdivision/amalgamation; the first three construction-extension years carried charges of 4%, 5% and 6% of total premium. Post-lease surrender deducted 30% of premium, and reserved farmer/functional-industrial allotments had five-year transfer restrictions. The brochure prints RERA UPRERAPRJ307764/03/2026; independent current RERA verification is not established. These original brochure facts were decoded on 24 October and retained with their historical dates; the currently retrieved brochure is unchanged.
