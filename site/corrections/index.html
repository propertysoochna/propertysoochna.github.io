Corrections and reporting errors

Report a possible error

Email propertysoochna@gmail.com with the Bulletin URL, the statement in question and the official notice or portal link that supports the correction. Do not include applicant personal information, account credentials or financial documents.

How material corrections are handled

  1. Recheck the disputed statement against the official source.
  2. Publish a correction explaining the old and corrected facts, the supporting source and the correction time; do not silently alter the public channel post.
  3. Update the affected web Bulletin with a visible correction note. If a PDF is reissued, use a revision suffix.

Where to check

Read correction notes in the affected Bulletin. For Delhi NCR, also check Join NCR free ↗. The Bulletin archive retains dated records. This page explains the policy; it is not a claim that no past errors or corrections exist.
