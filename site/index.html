Questions

Before you join

When is the bulletin published?

A complete verified bulletin is published every Monday in India Standard Time. Urgent booking alerts, deadline changes and material corrections are posted between Monday bulletins when needed.

Is PropertySoochna a government service?

No. It is an independent information publication and is not affiliated with any authority.

Do you accept application or booking money?

No. Property payments must be made only through the official authority portal or process linked in the bulletin.

Do you guarantee live inventory?

No. We state whether inventory was verified live, announced in a static list, or hidden behind authenticated login.

What does the monthly membership include?

You receive every Monday bulletin published while your membership is active, urgent corrections and alerts between bulletins, plus the month-end compilation.

Do I need a separate website login?

No custom PropertySoochna login is planned for the MVP. Telegram and the payment provider manage access. Checkout may still request contact details.
