Before you join.

What does Rs 9 include?

One paid region's scheme discoveries, monitoring and updates for a month. Each paid region is a separate subscription.

How do I get the updates?

Read Delhi NCR, Gurugram and Noida bulletins free on the website. Delhi NCR also has a free WhatsApp group. City editions reuse the relevant NCR findings and source-check dates; they are not separate fresh scans. For a paid region, request access from its page.

Which editions are free?

Delhi NCR, Gurugram and Noida are free. Noida includes Greater Noida and the Yamuna Expressway area. Other regional editions are Rs 9/month per region.

How do the NCR and city editions overlap?

Delhi NCR is the broad edition. Gurugram and Noida show the relevant local findings from the same research. Choose a city for a narrower read; the same opportunity may appear in both the city and NCR editions.

Are you a government service?

No. We are an independent information service. We do not sell property or process applications. Use the official authority portal to apply.
