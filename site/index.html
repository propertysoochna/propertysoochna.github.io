Questions

Before you join

When is the bulletin published?

A complete verified bulletin is published every Monday in India Standard Time. Urgent booking alerts, deadline changes and material corrections are posted between bulletins when needed.

Which authorities and opportunities do you cover?

We monitor HSVP/HUDA, GDA, MDA, YEIDA and DDA residential opportunities. We separately track DDA public flats and TCP Haryana's official e-Draw private-builder flats. More NCR authorities and categories are coming soon.

Is PropertySoochna a government service?

No. PropertySoochna is an independent information publication and is not affiliated with any government authority.

How do you handle availability and payments?

We label whether availability was verified live, announced in a static list or hidden behind login. Property application and booking payments must be made only through the official authority process linked in the bulletin.

How does Early Access work?

The first 100 members receive the Monday bulletin, urgent alerts and the public archive free. Future membership will be Rs 9/month; joining details will be shared before payments open.

Where should I follow PropertySoochna?
