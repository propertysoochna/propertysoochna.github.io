Who can book: an Indian citizen aged 18+ with PAN and a savings account. There is no existing-property restriction, and more than one flat may be booked.

Booking process

  1. Register on the official DDA Awaas Portal using PAN. Existing portal registrants do not pay the scheme fee again.
  2. From noon on 20 October, select a specific flat.
  3. Pay Rs 1 lakh within 15 minutes in one net-banking or card transaction.
  4. Check the portal for the Demand-cum-Allotment Letter, normally issued within a week.
  5. Pay the full price within 60 days of the letter to avoid interest.

Before paying: the brochure title says Pockets 3 and 4, while the annexure and circular say Pockets 3 and 6. Confirm the exact pocket and flat. Maintenance, Rs 2,000 water charge and freehold conversion are extra.

Indicative all-in: about Rs 15.70-15.76 lakh for a woman buyer or Rs 16.00-16.06 lakh for a man, before maintenance, water and conversion charges.
