Before you book: DDA Awas Nivesh

Government · DDA public flats

Awas Nivesh Yojana 2026

What and where?
2,516 ready-to-move LIG flats in Narela. Published offer, not a verified unsold balance.
Can I apply?

UPCOMING CONFIRMED Booking opens 20 October 2026.

Indian citizen, age 18+, with PAN and a savings account. No existing-property restriction; more than one flat may be booked.

Evidence: STATIC OFFER LIST ONLY. Live unit balances are login-gated.

What will I pay?

Upfront: Rs 1 lakh booking amount. Registration: Rs 2,500 including GST; existing portal registrants do not pay the scheme fee again.

Published price: about Rs 14.95–15.01 lakh. Indicative total: about Rs 15.70–15.76 lakh for a woman buyer or Rs 16.00–16.06 lakh for a man.

Extra: maintenance, Rs 2,000 water charge and freehold conversion. Maintenance and conversion amounts were not recorded; these totals exclude all three extras.

Safe timing?
Log in before 20 October 2026, noon IST. Selection holds a flat for only 15 minutes; pay Rs 1 lakh in one transaction. Scheme closes 31 January 2027, or earlier if inventory ends. Exact closing time was not recorded.
What could go wrong?
SOURCE CONFLICT: the brochure title says Pockets 3 and 4; the annexure and circular say Pockets 3 and 6. Confirm the exact pocket and flat before paying. Pay the full price within 60 days of the Demand-cum-Allotment Letter to avoid interest.
What should I do?

Read the brochure, confirm the flat and charges, then use the official portal. Do not treat the offer count as current stock.

Official scheme page · DDA brochure
Checked / changed?
19 October 2026, 9:08 PM IST. Brochure now available; eligibility, payment and transfer terms verified.
Five booking steps
  1. Register on the official DDA Awaas Portal using PAN. Existing portal registrants do not pay the scheme fee again.
  2. From noon IST on 20 October 2026, select a specific flat.
  3. Pay Rs 1 lakh within 15 minutes in one net-banking or card transaction.
  4. Check the portal for the Demand-cum-Allotment Letter, normally issued within a week.
  5. Pay the full price within 60 days of the letter to avoid interest.
