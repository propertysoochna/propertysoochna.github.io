Awas Nivesh Yojana 2026

Property
Government / DDA public flats; ready-to-move LIG
Location
Narela. The brochure names Pockets 3 and 4; the annexure and circular name Pockets 3 and 6.
Price
Published price about Rs 14.95–15.01 lakh. Indicative total about Rs 15.70–15.76 lakh for a woman buyer or Rs 16.00–16.06 lakh for a man. These totals exclude maintenance, the Rs 2,000 water charge and freehold conversion; maintenance and conversion amounts were not recorded.
Upfront payment
Rs 1 lakh booking amount. Registration is Rs 2,500 including GST; existing portal registrants do not pay the scheme fee again.
Who can apply
Indian citizens aged 18+, with PAN and a savings account. No existing-property restriction; more than one flat may be booked.
Dates
Booking opens 20 October 2026, noon IST. Closes 31 January 2027, or earlier if inventory ends. Exact closing time was not recorded.

Offered units: 2,516 LIG flats, not a verified unsold balance.

Change: Brochure available; eligibility, payment and transfer terms recorded in this issue.

Important: Confirm the conflicting pocket numbers and exact flat before paying. A selection is held for only 15 minutes; pay Rs 1 lakh in one transaction. Pay the full price within 60 days of the Demand-cum-Allotment Letter to avoid interest. Offered inventory is not remaining stock; live unit balances require login.

Official scheme page / DDA brochure
More details
  • Register on the official DDA Awaas Portal using PAN. Existing registrants do not pay the scheme fee again.
  • Log in before noon IST on 20 October 2026 and select a specific flat once booking opens.
  • Pay Rs 1 lakh within 15 minutes in one net-banking or card transaction.
  • Check for the Demand-cum-Allotment Letter, normally issued within a week.
  • Pay the full price within 60 days of that letter to avoid interest.
